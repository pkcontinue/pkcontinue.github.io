About Me

I am Yezhao Xu (Julius), currently at Alibaba Taotian. Previously, I worked with the Tencent Hunyuan team on large language model post-training. I received my M.S. in Applied Mathematics from Nanjing University in 2025.

My interests lie in agentic AI, LLM post-training, and tool use, particularly reward modeling, deep search, and evaluation.

Feel free to reach out at zyx20002018 [at] 163.com.
